📦 Free Sample Haul — modal demo

Reveals the original retail value of free ($0) TikTok Shop samples — the membership-club pitch that you can replace household expenses with free product. The numbers are mock (samples-mock.js): ~1,500 samples ≈ $75,000 retail across two accounts in one week.

Inside the modal: a style switcher (SmashTV · Showcase) and a “Just the numbers” checkbox (its preference is remembered). Press Esc or click the backdrop to close. Toggle your OS “reduce motion” setting to see the no-animation path.
