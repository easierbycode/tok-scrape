Sample Orders → Content

Each free / sample product the creator received, matched to the videos and LIVEs made for it. The order list is the to-do list; the content analytics show what's been posted — and where the gaps are.

Step 1 · collect
📦 Sample order list
scrape-order-list.js captures every $0 / sample order — the products a creator needs to make content for.
→
Step 2 · collect
🎬 Video & LIVE analytics
Affiliate export (tiktok-affiliate-export in Graylog) ties each content piece to the product it sold, with GMV & commission.
→
Step 3 · correlate
🔗 Match by product
Join content → product → sample order by product name. Empty join = a sample that still needs content.
